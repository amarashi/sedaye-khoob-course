## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Does this look AI-generated? Above the fold, no. Below it, yes — and the split is the story.**

**LLM assessment.** The hero is genuinely distinctive: a 3D calligraphic wordmark under a gold spotlight with five layered `drop-shadow()` filters tracing its silhouette (styles.css:571-582), an aurora halo drifting through hue over 18s, a tuxedoed conductor rising on a lit podium with three ostad orbs arced around him, notes drifting in the dark. ## Overall Impression

You have one of the best hero sections I've seen on a course page, attached to a checkout that cannot be trusted or completed with confidence. The brand work is real and it is being wasted: the page spends its most technically ambitious component — a 3D perspective carousel in a full-width night band — on the single piece of content that actively destroys credibility, while the one asset that would build it (the teacher) sits fully written in `content/site.json` with no markup to render it.

The biggest opportunity is a swap, not a redesign: put the teacher and the price where the fake testimonials are.

## What's Working

1. **The hero is a genuine brand asset.** The luminous shadow technique on the wordmark (a warm-white rim, a gold cast below, a wide ambient bloom — styles.css:571-582) is something almost nobody reaches for, and combined with the drifting aurora halo and the pointer parallax it earns "a room where music is happening" honestly. It delivers PRODUCT.md's "excitement in the first ten seconds" exactly as briefed.

2. **The instrument-orb system is a real alternative to icons.** Radial fill, hairline border, a rotated inner ring at 15% inset, image at 86%, hover lift, a 6.8s float on the active one. It gives rendered illustration a consistent home so the page never shows a photo in a rectangle. It just isn't used enough — three sections revert to icons and bare images.

3. **Reduced motion is implemented properly, and structure is clean.** `styles.css:1240-1248` keeps `[data-reveal]` visible with `!important`, kills the orb float, and `script.js` bails out of GSAP entirely. Independently verified: exactly one `h1`, 24 headings with zero skipped levels, zero images missing `alt`, zero console errors, 22 requests all static and successful.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The page never states a price.**
No price exists anywhere in `content/site.json` — the field named `checkout.priceLabel` contains the words "دوره آنلاین" — and none appears in the rendered page. A visitor must enter a real name and mobile number and be redirected to ZarinPal before learning what the course costs. For an audience whose one blocker is trust in an unfamiliar seller, hiding the price is the strongest "this is a scam" signal available, and it is worse than any number you could print.
*Why it matters:* success is defined as completed purchases; this is the single largest leak in the funnel.
*Fix:* put the number in the checkout summary card at h2 scale, in Persian numerals with تومان, above the benefits list; repeat it inside the form card immediately above the submit button so it is on screen when the button is pressed; add one line — "بعد از این دکمه به درگاه پرداخت زرین‌پال می‌روید" — beneath it.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable clarify checkout`

**[P0] The only proof on the page is fabricated — and the real proof is sitting unrendered.**
The testimonials are "امیر داوینچی" (Amir Da Vinci) and **"مارمالاد فسقلیان"** — names that read as jokes to a Persian speaker. The contact block ships `hello@sedayekhoob.example` and two Australian numbers (+61) on a page for an Iranian audience. The DOM itself confesses it: `aria-label="نظرهای نمونه هنرجوها"` — *sample* reviews. Meanwhile `content/site.json` contains a complete `about` block — the studio's story, method statement and stats — `server/content-renderer.js` already wires `about.paragraphs` and `about.stats`, and **`index.html` has no about section at all**, so none of it renders. The nav label `navigation.links.about` is defined and unused.
*Why it matters:* this is the exact objection you identified as the conversion blocker, and the page currently answers it with evidence that looks forged. Fake proof costs more credibility than an empty section.
*Fix:* delete the testimonial band and give that night register to the teacher — portrait in the orb frame, name, real training and playing background, in their own voice, built from the unused `about` content. Replace the contact grid with one real Iranian mobile as a `https://wa.me/98…` link, explicitly labelled WhatsApp — PRODUCT.md's secondary CTA, which does not exist on the page today (the only "واتساپ" entry is a `tel:` link to Australia).
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable craft بخش مدرس` then `/impeccable clarify contact`

**[P1] The checkout form cannot fail gracefully.**
`novalidate` is set and the submit handler performs no validation whatsoever, so `required` on `#fullName` and `#mobile` is inert and an empty form POSTs to `/api/orders`. The mobile field has no pattern despite the server enforcing `^09\d{9}$`. Every failure renders the same generic sentence with no indication of which field was wrong and no focus move. A network error surfaces `error.message` — English "Failed to fetch" — to a Persian reader. `finally` re-enables the submit button during `location.assign`, so a double-click creates a second order.
*Why it matters:* a nervous beginner handing a phone number to an unknown seller, who gets silently rejected, does not try again.
*Fix:* validate `fullName` non-empty and `mobile` against `^09\d{9}$` on blur and submit; render errors inside the `.field` beside the offending input and move focus there; move "(اختیاری)" from the email placeholder into the label; keep the button disabled through the redirect; never surface a raw `Error.message` to the user.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable harden checkout`

**[P1] On a phone there is no way to buy for ~6,000px — and the footer breaks outright at 390px.**
`styles.css:1216` hides `.nav-cta` entirely below 640px. The mobile page is 9,488px tall, so between the hero CTA and the checkout section there is no purchase affordance at all, for an audience described as "usually on a phone." Separately, `.contact-grid` stays a three-column grid at 390px: each card is 103px wide while `hello@sedayekhoob.example` renders 221px, so the email string overlaps the adjacent phone number. `body` overflows its client width by 16px at 390px, hidden only by `overflow-x: clip`. Nine interactive elements are under 44×44 at 390px, including the 40×40 theme toggle and four 9×9 carousel dots.
*Why it matters:* text overflowing its container is an absolute ban, and it happens in the footer — the last thing a visitor reads.
*Fix:* replace the hidden nav CTA with a bottom-fixed gold pill that appears once the hero leaves the viewport, carrying the price and خرید دوره; collapse `.contact-grid` to one column below ~700px and let the address wrap; bring the theme toggle to 44×44.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable adapt mobile`

**[P2] The page's central claim is never demonstrated, and its headline is printed twice.**
**شنیدن قبل از حفظ کردن** — the line PRODUCT.md names as the 10-second takeaway — appears once, as the 19px `<h3>` of the first of three identical feature cards, under a generic squiggle icon, roughly 3,000px down. The hero instead runs "تئوری موسیقی، شبیهِ تجربه‌ی نواختن" — which the very next section then reprints verbatim as its `<h2>`, so the first thing a scrolling visitor experiences is the page repeating itself. And for a course whose entire positioning is that theory is *audible*, there is not one `<audio>` element on the page.
*Why it matters:* the differentiator is asserted, never shown; and the duplicate headline reads as padding at the exact moment the visitor is deciding whether to keep scrolling.
*Fix:* promote شنیدن قبل از حفظ کردن to the hero, give the course section a headline that advances the argument instead of repeating one, and add a playable interval or chord beside the نت‌ها module — the highest-value single addition available to this page.
*Suggested command:* `/impeccable typeset index.html` then `/impeccable delight curriculum`

## Minor Observations

- **Content visibility is gated on ScrollTrigger.** `script.js:498` runs `gsap.fromTo(el, {opacity: 0, y: 28}, {…scrollTrigger})` over all 20 `[data-reveal]` elements — including the checkout form — which overrides the correct CSS default at `styles.css:1164`. I verified both halves: a normally-scrolling visitor **does** see everything (form opacity 0 → 1, nothing left hidden after a full scroll), but a renderer that doesn't fire scroll events sees the course, curriculum, method, outcomes and the entire checkout form as blank space. The full-page capture at `.playwright-mcp/desktop-light-full.png` shows exactly that. Social-preview screenshotters, some crawlers, and print are all in that category. Add a failsafe that clears the inline opacity after ~2s, or switch to an IntersectionObserver whose hidden state lives in CSS.
- Dark mode: `--color-surface-2` is a raw `rgb(90, 5, 5)` at ~1.6× the chroma of the surface beside it, so `.course-instrument` tiles read hotter than their surroundings. `--color-bg` and `--color-surface` are raw `oklab()`. DESIGN.md flags all three; they're still there.
- Dark mode: the fourth testimonial card takes `--crimson-500`, the same family as `.form-message.is-error`. A resting testimonial and an error state share a palette.
- "© 2026" in Latin digits, in a display context, against the system's own Persian-numeral rule — and a Gregorian year for an Iranian audience.
- The three contact *values* are marked up as `<h3>`. Two `<h2>` siblings sit inside `#checkout`.
- `.hero-orchestra` is `aria-hidden` with all orb images `alt=""` — correct, but it means the brand's entire visual argument is invisible to assistive tech, leaving a headline, a lead and three unverified numbers.
- Three `<svg>` elements have neither `aria-hidden`, `aria-label`, nor a `<title>`.
- No skip link; the first tab stop is the brand wordmark.
- The build artifacts `robots: noindex, nofollow` and a `localhost` canonical are correct for the Pages preview — worth confirming they stay environment-gated.

## Questions to Consider

2. The brief says trust is the *only* conversion problem. The page spends its best real estate and its most ambitious component on the one piece of content that destroys trust. What happens if the teacher goes in that slot — face, name, training, in the orb frame, in their own words?
